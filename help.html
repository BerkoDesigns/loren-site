My approach

Gentle, practical, and built around you

No two people are alike, so I don't use one method for everyone. I draw from several well-researched approaches and choose what fits you.

  • Trauma-focused therapy, including TF-CBT, Cognitive Processing Therapy, and exposure therapy
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
  • Schema therapy and inner child healing
  • Psychodynamic, Gestalt, and person-centered work
  • Solution-focused brief therapy and strength-based approaches

Welcoming to everyone

I welcome clients of all backgrounds, including LGBTQ+ clients. Sessions are online, so you can join from somewhere comfortable.
